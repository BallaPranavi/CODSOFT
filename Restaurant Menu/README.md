# 🍽️ Task 3: Restaurant Menu UI Design

This is my submission for *Task 3* of the *UI/UX Design Internship at CodSoft*.
The task was to design a *modern and user-friendly restaurant menu mobile app* using *Figma*.

## 🎯 Objective

To design a simple and engaging food ordering interface that provides:

- ✅ Easy navigation
- ✅ Clear food categories
- ✅ Attractive food presentation
- ✅ Simple product browsing
- ✅ Smooth ordering experience

## 🖼️ Design Overview

The restaurant menu app consists of four main screens:

### 1. Start Screen

- Attractive food image to create visual appeal
- "Enjoy your Food" welcome message
- Clear **Get Started** button
- Simple and minimal layout for easy entry into the app

### 2. Menu Screen

- Search bar for quickly finding food items
- Food categories including **All, Pizza, Burger, and Dessert**
- Promotions section highlighting special offers
- Popular food items with images, prices, and ratings
- Bottom navigation for easy access to different sections

### 3. Pizza Details Screen

- Large product image of the Cheese Pizza
- Pizza name, price, preparation time, and rating
- Short product description
- Add-ons section for customizing the order
- Favourite and **Add to Cart** options

### 4. Burger Details Screen

- Large product image of the Chicken Burger
- Burger name, price, preparation time, and rating
- Product description
- Add-ons for customizing the order
- Favourite and **Add to Cart** options
